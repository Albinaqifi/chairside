**For the reviewer and tester: look hard at**
- The provider's row lock (`FOR UPDATE`) is how two bookings take turns; the exclusion constraint stays the last guard, so a writer that skips the lock (E01-T06's move) gets `internal`, not `overlap`.
- The overlap query counts visits booked with `overlap_ok`, which the constraint ignores.
- `start_skipped`, a code the task did not name: a local time the clocks skip is refused, not stored an hour later as PostgreSQL reads it; a time that comes twice is stored as the second (standard time). Tested in both zones.
- The drawer's choices are read once, when it opens (`BookingChoices`): a read on use changes nothing in it, and a drawer left open on today past midnight books the day its title names.
- Escape: Radix listens on the document in the capture phase, before the field, so the drawer's `onEscapeKeyDown` closes the list first.
- The guard compares the events' own time stamps (the opening's focus event, the press), never the handler's time: a busy page held a press back past 500 ms once (WebKit, full suite). A press stamped before the opening counts. The three browsers stamp both on one clock (checked); Playwright's fake clock does not move it.
- The e2e double-click check runs at 1920 px: at 1280 New appointment is under the drawer, where the second click changes nothing. Its 150 ms part asserts only when the page got the two presses under 400 ms apart, else it adds an annotation.
- The treatment's label points (`for`) at the chips' group, a div: the group is named by `aria-labelledby` and described by its problem, without `aria-invalid` (not allowed on a group), so StaffForm's focus does not go there.
- A suggestion is picked on the press (`mousedown`, prevented, the focus kept); a disabled one is `aria-disabled`.
- The action calls `refresh()` and `revalidatePath('/patients')` (the patient list's next visit changed); its answer carries the provider's id, for the column turned on.
