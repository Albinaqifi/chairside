**What was settled**
- **Owner, 2026-10-13 (D-113):** only active patients can be booked. An archived or deceased patient
  shows in the drawer's suggestions with their label and cannot be picked; the action refuses one too.
  E02 sends nothing to either (E02-T05), so their booking would get no reminder, silently.
- **E01-T05 split in two** (architect, D-113): as one task about 850 lines of product code by estimate.
  This task books an existing patient at a free time from New appointment; E01-T05b (Planned, refined
  in this task's Done pass) adds the overlap warning with "Book anyway" and one booking per click, a
  click on an empty slot and a new patient in one go. An overlap is refused here, the race included,
  with one message: the slice books end to end without the override.
- **The nested-form guard moves to E01-T06** (D-113): no form sits inside another's here, and
  E01-T05b's new patient is fields of the booking form. E01-T06's cancel dialog over the move form is
  the first case; its Do item and criterion say so.
- **A treatment is required** (architect, `scope-v1.md`): none is chosen until one is picked (the demo
  starts on Checkup), its name is the title (`appointments.title` is required) and its length the
  default. A clinic without treatment types books nothing until E01-T09 (the seed's second clinic has
  none: say so in the drawer, as the calendar says it has no providers).
- **The drawer books on the day the calendar shows** (the demo's has no day field); its starts are the
  grid's quarter hours, its lengths the demo's (15–120) plus the treatment's own, all the browser's own
  selects, so no number field is typed (E01-T09 keeps the first number fields' texts).
- **It opens from the page's state, never a navigation** (architect, from the E01-T04b tester's
  `speed` line): React holds a navigation's transition while a read on use is on its way.
- **Risk normal:** a new `staffAction` and module function; no wrapper, `StaffForm`, policy,
  permission, table, grant or migration change (migration 0002 gives the app role INSERT on
  `appointments`, and UPDATE on `providers`, which a row lock needs).
- **Size:** about 500 lines with comments by estimate: the module (the booking, the treatment types,
  ~130), the action and the patient check (~70), the drawer (~250), the calendar's button and state
  (~40), the double-click guard (~25), texts. E01-T04b came to 570 against 200–350 and took one

**For the builder: what is already there** (checked on `main` at `ea1e68e`)
- **The calendar** (`src/app/(staff)/calendar/`): `page.tsx` reads `readCalendarDay.forPage({ day })`
  and renders `CalendarScreen` keyed by the clinic (D-098), so the drawer inside it starts again for
  another clinic. `actions.ts`: `readCalendarDay`, a `staffRead` under `calendar.manage` answering
  `CalendarRead` (`{ view, ownProviderId }`, `day.ts`). The treatment types can ride on that read, so
  the drawer opens without a request. `day.ts`'s `Column` has no `active`: `layoutDay` keeps an
  inactive provider with an appointment that day, which is no choice for a booking. `day-calendar.tsx`
  draws the header (Previous, Today, Next, the title, the chips, the count); New appointment goes there
  (demo line 237). The hour labels format a minute of the day in UTC (`HOUR_LABEL`): a start's label is
  a local time, never a moment, so format it the same way, never in the clinic's zone.
- **The read on use** (`calendar-screen.tsx`, `read-on-use.ts`, D-108) listens on the window: a pointer
  moved or a key pressed inside the open drawer reads the day again every 30 s. The view object then
  changes under the drawer: nothing in the drawer may be derived from `view` in a way that resets what
  was typed or chosen (a criterion). Its `startTransition(async …)` also holds `StaffForm`'s send,
  another async transition, until it ends: Book's answer may wait for a read on its way (E01-T11 times
  it). An answer for an earlier render is dropped (`read.after`), and a day is as old as its read
  (`readTimes`).
- **The block at once:** Next.js 16's `refresh()` from `next/cache`, called in a server action, sends
  the page again in the action's answer (`node_modules/next/dist/docs/01-app/03-api-reference/04-functions/refresh.md`);
  `createPatient` uses `revalidatePath('/patients')` (`patients/new/actions.ts`). The page's new
  `initial.view` replaces a read on use started before the booking. A booking also changes a patient's
  next visit on the patient list, which an earlier copy reached by Back still holds.
- **`StaffForm`** (`src/app/_lib/staff-form.tsx`, D-092): one submit button (`submitLabel` may change
  with the choices), `refusals` as texts above it, `onDone(value)` after the toast (closes the drawer,
  turns the provider's column on), `doneMessage` a string the drawer can compose. It keeps every field
  after done: the drawer closes instead. Its sent-once guard drops a second submit; a second request
  would also be refused as an overlap of the first. A field's problem shows at the `StaffField` whose
  `name` the schema names: the patient field is the `StaffField` of `patientId`, and the search box
  itself has no `name`, so its words are no field of the booking (D-095). Enter in a text field sends
  its form (implicit submission): the patient field's Enter picks a suggestion instead.
- **The patient search:** `searchPatients` (`patients/actions.ts`, a `staffRead` for every member, the
  words in the body) answers up to 50 rows with `status` and the phone as written (`listPatients`);
  `sendSearch` (`patients/search-answer.ts`) turns the answer and a failed call into the list's
  answers; `patient-list.tsx` waits 250 ms after the last key and drops an answer to an earlier search
  (its `ticket`). Its status label is `<Badge variant={row.status}>`. No function tells the action
  whether a patient is active: `patientNames` gives names only; the patients module needs one
  (`patients` imports `scheduling`, so the scheduling module cannot ask it).
- **The scheduling module** writes nothing yet: `appointments.ts` (`appointmentsOnDay`, its `AT TIME
  ZONE` the pattern for turning a local time into a moment), `providers.ts`, `schema.ts` (the exclusion
  constraint is migration 0002's; it ignores `overlap_ok` rows, cancelled ones and no-shows). Nothing
  reads `treatment_types` yet; the seed's ten are Harbour Street's only. A row lock is `.for('update')`
  (`tenancy/hours.ts`, `clinic-details.ts`); a lock wait fails after 5 s. `clinicPlace(tx)` gives the
  zone (tenancy).
- **The drawer** (`src/ui/sheet.tsx`, E00-T04e): `SheetContent` needs a `title`, takes a
  `description`, spreads the rest onto Radix's Content (`onPointerDownOutside`, `onInteractOutside`,
  `onOpenAutoFocus` are where the double-click guard goes, in `dialog.tsx` too; `sheet-dialog.test.ts`
  tests them). Radix focuses the first control inside: the close button comes after the children, so
  the patient field is first if nothing focusable is above it. `NativeSelect` is the browser's select.
- **Tests:** `tests/integration/appointments-overlap.test.ts`, `scheduling-constraints.test.ts` and
  `scheduling-fixtures.ts` (rows, `insertProvider`, `insertClinicRefs`); the days the clocks change
