**For the builder: what is already there** (checked on `main` at `ec2e1ca`; E01-T01a adds the
tables and the provider link)
- `SEED_FILES` (`src/db/seed/seed-files.ts`) lists the seed files in order. A file is data:
  `tables` (rows written as listed, as the migration role) and `people`. `seedDatabase(urls)`
  (`index.ts`) takes no day, and `insertRow()` only inserts (`on conflict (id) do nothing`): the
  week around the day and the membership links each need a change there; E00-T05a's file stays as
  it is.
- `seeded.ts`: the fixed ids (`019a0000-5eed-7000-8000-…`, version 7, variant 8), the seeded
  people and memberships. The dentist's and hygienist's memberships are `harbourStreetDentist` and
  `harbourStreetHygienist`; a seeded front desk can be the seeded appointments' `created_by`.
- `fake-data.ts`: `FIRST_NAMES` and `LAST_NAMES` (eight each in Latin and in Cyrillic, enough for
  40 patients), `fakePhone(n)`, `EMAIL_DOMAIN`, `isFakeName()` (a listed name, or a first and a
  last name in one script) and `fakeDataProblems()`, which reads every key holding "name":
  `short_name`, `guardian_name` and a treatment's `name` among them. `fake-data.test.ts` runs it
  over every seed file.
- The demo: providers at lines 1369–1373 (name, short name, chair, kind, hue), patients at
  1386–1404 (their shape only: ages, one without a phone), treatments with lengths at 1518, the day
  from 08:00 to 18:00 at 1519 (`START`, `END`).
- `pnpm db:seed` runs `src/db/seed/index.ts` with tsx; `pnpm db:reset` migrates, then seeds.
  `tests/integration/seed.test.ts` is E00-T05a's test of the seed.
- Trap: a test that seeds the test database leaves its rows for the other test files of the run;
  they make their own clinics, but a test that counts rows across clinics would see 40 more
  patients.
