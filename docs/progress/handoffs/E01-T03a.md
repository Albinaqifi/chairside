## Builder — 2026-10-28

**What I built.** The patient profile at `/patients/[id]`: header (name, age, phone, email, "Patient since", a pill for archived or deceased, the guardian line), two columns (details and the note "Not a clinical record"; next appointment and the history of up to 20 completed visits). It is an audited read: each render writes one `patient.viewed` row (the patient's id and the entity, nothing else); a non-UUID, an unknown id and another clinic's patient are a 404 and write nothing. The name in a patient list row is the link (`prefetch={false}`, so a prefetch cannot write a row nobody opened). No Book button, no Messages, Communication, Care plan or Treatment plan.
