T06b keeps the end-to-end slice (a block opens, a status changes, the grid follows); T06e takes the rest and comes before T06c, whose move form is the first with a dialog inside it.
- **The buttons are computed in the module,** by `appointmentsOnDay(tx, day, timeZone, now)` returning each visit's `actions`, from the state machine for a staff actor and **one shared function of facts** that `changeAppointmentStatus` also calls (today inline in `status-change.ts`). Alternatives rejected: the client re-deriving them (it cannot import the module and would copy the table); showing every button and letting the action refuse (a No-show that always fails before the start is a trap at a busy desk).
- **No-show is offered once the visit has started** (`startsAt <= now` of the read). The read is at most 30 seconds old on a press (read on use, D-108), and the press itself brings a new one, so a button that appears a minute late shows within a second; the action still refuses `too_early`.
- **Each status button is a `StaffForm` of its own,** hidden `appointmentId` and `action`, as Settings' Make inactive and Up/Down are (`providers.tsx`): no `StaffForm` change, the toast and the refusals as everywhere else. The drawer stays open after a change.
- **Texts** the architect proposes: In the chair, Done, No-show, Confirm, Undo in the chair, Undo done; the builder may adjust them.
- **Cancel has no default for "who cancelled"** (T06e): a wrong default is a wrong record that a later message may read.
