**What was settled**
- **The Day field is in every booking**, not only Book next visit's (the outline's recommendation): the drawer then never books on a day it does not show, and one field has one behaviour. `scope-v1.md` says so (D-126 already put the field in the booking drawer). No decision entry: no document was untrue, no dependency added.
- **The read of another day is shared with the move form** (one hook, `pointed-day.ts`), so the last-answer-wins logic is written once. If the extraction costs more than about 40 changed lines in `appointment-move.tsx`, or a test must change, copy it instead and write a `none` line.
- **The button shows for every visit status;** the server decides about the patient (`patient_not_bookable` is an existing refusal text).
- **The calendar keeps the day it shows** after a booking on another day, as after a move; the toast names the day (`bookedOn`) when it differs. Taking the calendar to the booked day is out of scope.
- **The day the drawer opens on is the calendar's:** the visit's own day, but for a visit that runs past midnight. The length is 30 until a treatment is picked; no treatment is chosen (scope).
- No owner question; nothing here is on agent rule 4's list. The owner tries it when built: Book next visit from a drawer, change the day to one weeks ahead, look at the box and at Book.

**For the builder: what is already there** (read on `main` at `b4bd1cf`; no probe was run)
- `new-appointment.tsx`: `NewAppointment` holds `opening = { count, choices }` and keys `BookingDrawer` by `count`; `openOn(slot)` makes the choices with `bookingChoices(view, treatmentTypes, shown, now, slot)`; the handle is `{ openAt }`, guarded by `withinDoubleClick(closedAt, at)`. `BookingDrawer` holds `Booking` (patient, newPatient, searchText, treatmentId, start, length, providerId), started from `choices.firstStart` and `choices.firstProviderId`. In `BookingForm` the day is `<input type="hidden" name="day" value={choices.day}>`; the warnings are `view.day === choices.day ? bookingWarnings(view, booking) : NO_WARNINGS`; `anyway = overlaps.length > 0`; the starts come from `choices.starts` and the providers from `choices.providers`, both fixed at opening. After this task all of that follows the day typed.
- `appointment-move.tsx` is the pattern (`MoveFields`): `pointed`, `other`, `again`, the `latest` ticket in an effect that calls `readAgain(readCalendarDay, clinicId, pointed)` inside `startTransition`, `pointedDay(pointed, held, other, place.day)`, `startChoicesWith(view.grid, move.start)`, `bookableProviders(view.columns)`, `movesAnyway(…)`, and the line `couldNotCheck` in a `role="status"` div. `move-choices.ts` has the pure parts and their tests.
- `bookAppointment` (`actions.ts`): `day: z.string().refine(isShownDay)`; nothing changes there. A half-typed date arrives as `''` and shows at the field as `invalid_input`.
- `appointment-drawer.tsx`: the foot is a `SheetFooter` with the Patient profile `Link` (`flex-1`, `prefetch={false}`); `onCloseAutoFocus` calls `focusBlock(block.id)`. `AppointmentBlock` has `patientId` and `patient` (the names); `Column` has `providerId` and `active`. `calendar-screen.tsx` has `booking = useRef<NewAppointmentHandle>`, `setOpenId`, and renders the drawer as `opened && <AppointmentDrawer …>`.
- `PickedPatient` is `{ id, firstName, lastName }` (`patient-field.tsx`); a picked patient is replaced by typing (`if (picked) onPick(null)`), so "not locked" is how it already behaves.
- Traps: (1) Closing the appointment drawer and opening the booking drawer in one press: Radix's focus return (`focusBlock`, the sheet's unmount focus) can take the focus from the new drawer; check by keyboard.
