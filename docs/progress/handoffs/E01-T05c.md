**For the builder: what is already there** (checked on `main` at `9c202e0`)
- **The guard** (`src/ui/double-click-guard.ts`): `DOUBLE_CLICK_MS` (500), `withinDoubleClick()` and
  `useDoubleClickGuard()`, which keeps the opening's moment from Radix's `onOpenAutoFocus` and drops,
  within half a second of it, a pointer down outside (the overlay) and a pointer's press of the corner
  Close. Each moment is an event's `timeStamp`, never the time a handler runs (a busy page holds one
  back). `sheet.tsx:93` and `dialog.tsx:86` take it and spread `guard.content` after the caller's
  props, `guard.close` on Close: extend it there, and the drawer and the dialog get the press inside
  alike. Today only the components page (`/dev/components`) has a dialog.
- **Keeping the focus:** preventing a press's `mousedown` keeps the focus where it is; the patient
  field's suggestions do so (`calendar/patient-field.tsx:236`), and E01-T05a's fix round 2 saw the
  dropped press move the focus (its Discovered line 1). Which event opens a native select's list,
  moves the focus or presses a button differs by browser: the three-browser check is where it shows.
- **The drawer's state** (`calendar/new-appointment.tsx:49–95`): `NewAppointment` holds `open` and
  `opening` (a count and the choices); `openChange(true)` reads `bookingChoices(view, treatmentTypes,
  shown, now)` and keys `BookingDrawer` by the count, so each opening is a new booking, which starts
  from `choices.firstStart` and `choices.firstProviderId` (lines 126–132). `CalendarScreen`
  (`calendar-screen.tsx:120–153`) renders `DayCalendar` and hands it `NewAppointment` as the header's
  `newAppointment`: the grid's click has to reach that state.
- **The grid** (`calendar/day-calendar.tsx:289–317`): each column is a `div` holding the shaded spans,
  the half-hour lines (`aria-hidden`) and a `ul` (`absolute inset-0`, named by the column's header) of
  `li` blocks, `AppointmentItem` and `BreakItem`, none clickable yet; the "now" line takes no pointer.
  A click on a block bubbles to the column: the demo stops it at the break (logic 1828), and E01-T06
  gives the visit's block its own click. `PX_PER_MINUTE` is 1.45. The grid runs in whole hours
  (`day.ts`, `gridSpan`), so each quarter from its top is one of `startChoices(view.grid)`
  (`booking-choices.ts:45`). `Column.active` is false for an inactive provider shown for a visit
  (`day.ts:117–120`); `bookableProviders()` leaves them out.
- **Trap (a probe, lint on stdin):** jsx-a11y's recommended rules run on `src/app` and `src/ui`
  (`eslint.config.mjs:249–258`), and no lint comment switches one off (D-077). `<div onClick>` fails
  (`click-events-have-key-events`, `no-static-element-interactions`), and so does `onMouseDown`;
  `role="presentation"` or `aria-hidden="true"` on the element passes, and so do `onPointerDown` and
  `onPointerUp`. `aria-hidden` on the column itself would hide its blocks' list from a screen reader;
  a layer of its own under the blocks would not.
- **The read on use** starts on any use of the page (a pointer's move or press, a key;
