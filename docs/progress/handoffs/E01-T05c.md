**What I built**
A click on an empty slot of an active provider's column opens the booking drawer at once, on that provider and the quarter hour under the pointer (the grid's first and last quarter, a shaded time too); a visit's or a break's block and an inactive provider's column open nothing. For half a second after the drawer or the dialog opens, a pointer's press in it or on its overlay is dropped: no focus moves, no select opens, its click reaches no control. A click on the grid within half a second of the drawer closing opens nothing, so a double click on Book whose answer closes the drawer first books once and opens no second drawer.

About 260 new lines of product code with comments, on the estimate. Tests: cases in `double-click-guard.test.ts`, `day-calendar.test.ts`, `booking-choices.test.ts`; five checks in `tests/e2e/booking.spec.ts` (June 2031 days).
