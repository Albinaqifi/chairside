**What was settled** (the architect's choices; the rule itself is the owner's, D-114)
- Any status to any status is allowed; only archived and deceased are refused while a visit is ahead. A visit
  ahead is booked, confirmed or arrived and not ended at `now` (cancelled, no-show and completed do not hold the chair).
- The lock order is the patient's row first, then the provider's: the edit `for update`, the booking's
  `patientStatus` `for share`, and the booking's provider lock after it. No deadlock because both sides take the patient first.
- The changed fields go into the audit row as values of an array (`{ fields: ['phone'] }`); the audit check refuses a
  personal field name as a key only.
- The flaky case (Discovered, E01-T09e, `message`) comes here (D-121): this task changes the locks the case races on.
