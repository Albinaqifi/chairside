- Traps: (1) **a visit across midnight.** A block is cut at midnight, so `block.from` and the shown day are not the visit's start; read the start and the day from `block.startsAt` in the clinic's zone (`useTimeZone()`, `clinicNow` in `booking-choices.ts` does `dayInZone` and `minuteInZone`), and the length as `endsAt - startsAt` in minutes. (5) **`nothing_changed`** is a refusal above the button, not a lint of the form. (6) **A read on use under the form** replaces the visit's place (another desk moved it): the form restarts from the new place and the typed change is lost; that is the intended behaviour (D-108), not a bug to fix.
