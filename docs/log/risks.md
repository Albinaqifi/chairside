# Risks

What could hurt the project, how likely, how bad, and what we do about it.
Add a risk when you see one; update "Status" when something changes. Never delete a row.

Likelihood / Impact: **H**igh, **M**edium, **L**ow.

| ID | Risk | L | I | What we do about it | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-01 | **Cross-clinic data leak.** One clinic sees another's patients. | L | H | Row-Level Security on every table plus app-level tenant context (D-003); meta-test on every table (D-016); isolation tests written before features; owner reviews every schema and auth change. | Albin | Open |
| R-02 | **Silent message failure.** A reminder never goes out and nobody knows. | M | H | Message rows planned before sending; skipped/failed always visible; nightly canary ("every appointment tomorrow has a reminder or a reason"); heartbeat on the worker (D-013). | Albin | Open |
| R-03 | **Wrong or duplicate messages.** Wrong time, wrong patient, sent twice, sent after cancellation. | M | H | Unique idempotency keys; send-time re-checks; pure, heavily tested timing functions; time-zone and DST test tables; owner reviews planner and dispatcher. | Albin | Open |
| R-04 | **Runaway sending.** A bug or a big import sends thousands of messages. | M | H | Per-clinic daily and monthly caps, kill switch, recall start gate (D-030); non-production allow-list (D-044). | Albin | Open |
| R-05 | **Real patient data in development.** Leaks through logs, tests, AI tools or a laptop. | M | H | Synthetic data only (rule 5); no production credentials on the dev machine; logs and error tracking strip personal data, verified by test. | Albin | Open |
| R-06 | **Unsafe AI aftercare text** reaches a patient. | L | H | Human approval always; only dentists approve (D-051); template library first; evals on every prompt or model change; strict output schema. | Albin | Open |
| R-07 | **Patient identity sent to the AI provider.** | L | H | Prompt built only from treatment facts (D-037); test with "canary" patient values that must never appear in a prompt. | Albin | Open |
| R-08 | **Scope creep toward full practice software** (charting, billing, insurance). | H | H | Written non-goals in `intent.md`; D-017; Q-02 decided before pilot conversations promise anything. | Albin | Open |
| R-09 | **Front desk finds booking slower than their old tool** and stops using it. | M | H | Booking flow tested with a real receptionist as early as milestone M1 (Q-09); keyboard-friendly forms; speed is an acceptance criterion on scheduling tasks. | Albin | Open |
| R-10 | **Messy import data** makes the first day with a clinic go badly. | H | M | Staging table, review screen, undo for 7 days; realistic messy fixture files in tests; budget real time for E04. | Albin | Open |
| R-11 | **Channel rules block or delay launch:** WhatsApp business verification, template approval, opt-in policy; SMS sender rules later. | M | M | Owner starts Meta verification now, in parallel with M0–M2; E03-T01 checks the rules from current documentation; fake adapter keeps the rest unblocked. | Albin | Open |
| R-12 | **Legal exposure.** Wrong consent basis for recalls, health details in SMS, missing agreements. | M | H | Safe defaults (D-027, D-028, D-040) stay in place; go-live gate in `security-privacy.md`. | Albin | Accepted for now: owner deferred the legal review on 2026-10-02 (D-052). Revisit at go-live. |
| R-13 | **One person runs everything.** Outage at night, illness, holiday. | H | M | Managed hosting and database; alerts; a runbook; jobs wait in the database if the worker is down, nothing is lost. | Albin | Open |
| R-14 | **Agents drift:** inconsistent code, invented APIs, widened scope, weakened tests. | H | M | Small tasks with acceptance criteria; rules; fresh-context review; CI gates; conventions in `CLAUDE.md`; handoff notes. | Albin | Open |
| R-15 | **Docs go stale**, agents build from outdated plans. | M | M | Docs change in the same change as the code (rule 11); reviewer checks it; board is the single status source. | Albin | Open |
| R-16 | **Patient link abuse.** Guessing, forwarding, scanners "clicking" confirm. | L | H | 128-bit hashed tokens, expiry, rate limits, GET never changes anything, minimal data on link-only pages (D-025, D-026). | Albin | Open |
| R-17 | **Shared phone numbers** (families) cause wrong merges or wrong opt-outs. | M | M | Phone alone is never a duplicate (D-033); opt-out is per link, tied to one patient. | Albin | Open |
| R-18 | **Dashboard numbers that cannot be defended** damage trust. | M | M | Measured facts only in v1 (D-034); every number has a written definition. | Albin | Open |
| R-19 | **Dependency on young libraries** (auth, queue). | L | M | Thin wrappers around each; standard Postgres underneath; decisions list what would make us switch. | Albin | Open |
| R-20 | **Costs surprise the clinic or the operator** (per-message channels above all). | M | M | Usage visible to the clinic; caps; pricing model decided before the pilot (Q-04). | Albin | Open |
| R-21 | **Time-zone and daylight-saving bugs.** | M | H | All times stored in UTC, clinic time zone only for display and planning; no clock calls in business logic; test suite run in two server time zones. | Albin | Open |
| R-22 | **Lost data.** Bad migration, deletion bug, host failure. | L | H | Point-in-time recovery; a restore drill before go-live; migrations reviewed by the owner; no destructive migration without a written plan. | Albin | Open |
| R-23 | **SMS cost multiplies with Cyrillic or `ë`/`ç`** (only once SMS is added; WhatsApp is unaffected). One such character cuts a part from 160 to 70 characters, so a reminder becomes 3 parts instead of 1–2. | H | M | Decided (D-054): every SMS converted to plain Latin letters; correct part counting (E02-T03) shown in the editor; caps (D-030); short link domain (Q-03). | Albin | Open |
| R-24 | **Switch-over pain.** Chairside replaces the old tool (D-050), but only patients are imported; future appointments would be re-typed by hand. | H | M | Task to be added in E04-T00: import or fast keying of upcoming appointments. | Albin | Open |
| R-25 | **Health data hosted outside the country.** North Macedonia is outside the EU; hosting in Frankfurt with a US-owned company may need legal steps. | M | H | Listed in Q-06 item 5 (deferred, D-052); everything runs from one Docker image, so the host can change (D-007). | Albin | Open |
| R-26 | **Aftercare waits for a dentist and expires.** With dentist-only approval (D-051), drafts not reviewed within 24 hours are never sent. | M | M | Bulk approve for template drafts; the panel is on the dentist's Today; count expired drafts in the results so the clinic sees it. Revisit after the pilot. | Albin | Open |
| R-27 | **The WhatsApp sender gets restricted.** Meta lowers quality ratings or blocks numbers after user reports; with one shared number (Q-18) every clinic is affected at once. | M | H | Opt-in confirmation recorded; opt-out honoured by link and by reply; frequency cap; email fallback is automatic (D-055); SMS can be enabled once built (E03-T10); per-clinic numbers as an option. | Albin | Open |
| R-28 | **WhatsApp onboarding lead time.** Business verification and template approval take days to weeks and block M3. | H | M | Owner starts now (board); templates submitted early in E03; fake adapter keeps development moving. | Albin | Open |
| R-29 | **Opt-in for WhatsApp.** Meta's policy requires the business to hold the patient's opt-in; imported patients may lack a recorded one. | M | M | Clinic confirms at onboarding and in the import wizard; consent rows per channel; legal review deferred (D-052), so this stays a conscious risk. | Albin | Open |
| R-30 | **Patients without WhatsApp are unreachable** until SMS exists (email fallback only). | M | M | `unreachable` skip creates a "needs a call" task; `whatsapp_status` visible on the profile; E03-T10 ready to build when needed. | Albin | Open |
